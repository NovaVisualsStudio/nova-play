Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

NOVA PLAY is a multilingual mini-games application. We respect your privacy. The current version works without requiring an account.

1. Information and local storage

The app stores your game best scores locally on your device using the app's local storage (SharedPreferences). This information is used only to display your progress. These scores are not sent to our servers.

2. Collection and sharing

The current version does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, does not require account registration, and does not collect or share personal information through the app. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include and use it only to respond to your enquiry.

3. Retention and deletion

Game scores remain on your device until you clear NOVA PLAY's app storage or uninstall the app. We do not maintain a remote account or score database for this version.

4. Children

NOVA PLAY is a general mini-games app and the current version does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians may contact us with privacy concerns.

5. Security

Game scores are stored locally. Their protection also depends on the security of your device and operating system. Keep your device protected and up to date.

6. Changes

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The latest revision date appears above.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Hamniat61@gmail.com.
